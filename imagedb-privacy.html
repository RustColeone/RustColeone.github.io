Privacy Policy

What the app does with your data, and every case where something leaves your device.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Summary

ImageDB has no account system, no analytics, no telemetry, and no cloud service operated by me. Your images, thumbnails and metadata are stored in the app's private storage on your own device. I cannot see your library and have no mechanism to retrieve it.

The app is not, however, completely airtight against all network activity. The sections below describe every case where something leaves your device. Please read section 4 in particular.

2. Data collection

I collect nothing. No personal information, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry, no device identifiers, no advertising ID gathered by me. There is no server to collect it to.

3. Device permissions

The app requests access to local storage in order to read images you import and to write its own database and thumbnails. It requests camera access only if you use the hold-to-capture feature. These permissions exist to make the app function; they are not used to survey your device.

4. Network activity

ImageDB's core features — importing, tagging, searching, browsing, editing, exporting — work fully offline. Data leaves your device only in the following cases.

4.1 Advertising

ImageDB shows ads via Google AdMob in two specific places:

  • Automatic banner. A small banner may appear when you proceed to the advanced, non-essential Panel Images combine and preview step. This is the app's only automatic ad placement. That small amount of income is what funds continued maintenance.
  • Rewarded ad. A separate "support me by watching an ad" button, which only ever runs when you tap it.

The rest of the app is ad-free. In both cases AdMob may collect certain device information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Ad requests are gated through the platform consent flow where required. Your images are never sent to the ad network.

4.2 Linked images

If you save an entry as a URL rather than importing the file, ImageDB requests that image from its source host each time it is displayed. This exposes an ordinary network request — including your IP address — to that host, exactly as visiting the image in a browser would. You can convert linked entries to offline copies from Backup & Data.

4.3 Android system backup

The app sets allowBackup="true" intentionally. If you enable Android's system backup, the operating system may include eligible ImageDB app-private data in your backup, stored under your own Google account. I do not enable that setting and do not operate the backup destination. You can disable it in your device settings.

4.4 Actions you take

Data also leaves the app whenever you deliberately export it: using the system share sheet, saving to your phone's gallery, copying to the clipboard, or writing a backup ZIP. Where that data goes afterwards is determined by the app or destination you chose.

5. Third-party services

Google AdMob, as described in section 4.1, is the only third-party service in the app. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, and no social logins. All libraries the app uses (SQLite/WASM, ZIP, GIF and PDF handling) are bundled inside the app and are not fetched from a CDN at runtime.

6. Children

ImageDB is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone — since I do not collect information at all.

7. Your data, your responsibility

Because there is no cloud copy, uninstalling the app or losing the device means losing the library. Use the built-in ZIP export to keep your own backups.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of your data changes, this policy will be updated along with it, and the date at the top will change.

Privacy Policy for ImageDB

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

ImageDB is a completely offline application. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information, user data, or analytics.

2. Device Permissions

To function properly, the app requires access to your device's local storage. This permission is used exclusively to display your images and media within the app. None of your photos, files, or data ever leave your device or get sent to any external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

This app does not connect to the internet and does not use any third-party services, tracking tools, or advertising networks.

4. Changes to this Policy

If we ever change how this app operates regarding your data, we will update this policy.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, you can contact me at: laplacengan@gmail.com

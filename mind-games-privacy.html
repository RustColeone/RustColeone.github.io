NeuroCamp

Privacy Policy

Short version: the app has no network access, so there is nothing to collect and nowhere to send it.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Summary

NeuroCamp is a collection of offline brain-training games. It has no account system, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no server component of any kind. It does not transmit anything about you anywhere.

2. Data collection

None. No personal information, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry, no device identifiers, no advertising identifiers. The games do not ask who you are, and there is no server that could receive an answer.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of state in local storage inside its own sandbox:

  • Your best scores and recent results, so a game can show whether you improved.
  • Your theme preference (dark or light).
  • Game settings such as difficulty or grid size.

This never leaves the device, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall NeuroCamp or clear its storage.

4. Device permissions

NeuroCamp requests no special permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or shared storage. The sound-based games (Chord Scout, Interval Birds, Rhythm Sync) synthesise audio on the device with the Web Audio API rather than playing recorded files, so the app needs no media access and downloads no sound assets. Nothing is ever recorded.

5. Advertising

NeuroCamp contains no advertising. There is no ad SDK bundled in the app, no banner, and no rewarded-video button. This is a deliberate difference from ImageDB, which is partly ad-supported. If that ever changes here, this section will be rewritten before the change ships, not after.

6. Third-party services

There are none. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no social logins, no ad networks. The app is built with Capacitor and its core Android plugins only; everything it needs is bundled at build time and nothing is fetched from a CDN at runtime.

7. Children

The games are simple and suitable for general audiences. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of your data changes, this policy will be updated along with it, and the date at the top will change.

9. Contact

Questions: laplacengan@gmail.com

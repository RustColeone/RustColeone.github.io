Privacy Policy for NeuroCamp

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

NeuroCamp is a completely offline application. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information, user data, game scores, or analytics. All game data remains solely on your device and never leaves it.

2. Device Permissions

The app requires no special device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or local storage. Sound-based games (Camp Chord Scout, Rhythm Sync Test) use on-device Web Audio synthesis — no audio is recorded or uploaded.

3. Third-Party Services

The app includes an optional "Support me by watching this ad" button. If you choose to interact with it, a rewarded ad is served via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. This is the only external service the app uses, and it is only activated by your explicit interaction. No other third-party services, tracking tools, or analytics are used.

4. Changes to this Policy

If we ever change how this app operates regarding your data, we will update this policy.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, you can contact me at: laplacengan@gmail.com
